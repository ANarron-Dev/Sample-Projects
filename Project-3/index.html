Our Story

Founded in 2026, Handcrafted & Co, began with a simple mission: to bridge the gap between sustainable coffee farming and your morning mug. We partner directly with small-family farms to ensure fair wages and select only the highest-quality Arabica beans.
Every batch is micro-roasted to unlock its unique, geographic flavor profile.
