Crockpot Beef Stroganoff

Hearty comfort food dish requiring minimal prep.

Ingredients

  • 1lb hamburger
  • 1 packet beefy onion soup mix
  • 1 can french onion soup
  • 2 cups beef broth
  • 1lb elbow noodles
  • 1 cup milk

Instructions

  1. Break up hamburger in the bottom of crockpot
  2. Mix together soup, soup mix, and beef broth,
    and pour over hamburger
  3. Cook on LOW for 5 1/2 hours
  4. Stir to break up hamburger
  5. Add noodles and milk
  6. Cook on LOW for 45 minutes
  7. Stir and serve
